Employee Attendance and Payroll App

Keep a daily attendance and payment record for employees in your shop, wholesale business or factory. Use the standalone app on your device, or set up cloud access if staff need their own logins.

  • Daily attendance
  • Advances & payments
  • Staff check-in

Keep a daily attendance routine

  1. Add employees with the salary and start details you use for your business.
  2. Record attendance on the day it happens. Review the register regularly, especially after a half day or leave, so you can correct mistakes before paying.
  3. Review pay and previous payments for the employee. Save the actual amount paid and check what is left to pay.

Keep advances visible before the next payday

The Windows Store listing includes employee advances, bonuses and deductions. If you give someone an advance partway through the month, record it against that employee so you can see it when you review the account at payday. Enter each payment once, using the actual date and amount.

Owner attendance and employee self check-in are different

An owner can record attendance locally without a sign-in. Staff using their own phones need a connected workspace. The upcoming build adds nearby check-in through Bluetooth or the manager’s QR code, with employee self check-in also available from Home for signed-in staff.

Preview the newer payroll workflow

The upcoming build works out pay from attendance and lets you record payments without closing a month first. It also adds employee working hours, weekly days off, holidays, an attendance register in PDF or Excel, and a monthly statement. See the attendance and payroll release preview to check availability.
