Free POS and Billing Software for Shops in Pakistan

Virtual Manager gives your counter fast billing, receipts in English and Urdu, and stock that updates with every sale. It runs on the Windows PC or Android phone you already have, with or without internet.

What does POS software cost in Pakistan?

Many POS systems for shops are sold with a licence fee, a yearly renewal or a monthly subscription, and some need a dedicated terminal. Virtual Manager is free to download and use. Billing, sales, purchases, inventory, expenses, payroll and PDF exports need no subscription, and the app runs on a normal Windows computer or Android phone.

Bill at the counter with Quick Sell

Retail mode includes Quick Sell for fast counter billing. Wholesale mode is kept separate for traders who sell to regular customers in bulk.

  • Sell for cash or on credit. Credit sales go straight into the customer’s khata with a running balance.
  • The app remembers the rate each customer last paid, so repeat billing is quicker.
  • Turn on sales tax when you need it, set your rate and show your registration number.
  • Stock levels update with every sale, and the cost of each sale comes from the batch it was taken from.

Receipts customers can read

Type customer and item names in Urdu and they print in Nastaliq next to your English text. Add your tax number, show the customer’s balance and finish the receipt with your own business stamp. You can design a stamp in the app or turn a photo of your real stamp into one. Share receipts straight from the app.

Stock and profit update with every sale

A POS that only adds up sales cannot tell you what you earned. Virtual Manager keeps each supplier purchase as its own stock batch and uses the oldest first (FIFO), so every sale is costed at what that stock really cost you. Live Overview then shows profit after stock cost and expenses for today, this month or any date range.

Let a salesman bill without seeing everything

Give staff their own access with a QR code or a one-time code. They do not need an email account. Choose a role so a salesman sees only sales, hide salaries from everyone but you, and require your approval before staff edits or deletions take effect. Staff access needs sign-in and cloud sync.

How to choose POS software for your shop

Before you pay for any POS system, check it against the way your shop actually works:

  1. Does it keep billing when the internet is down? A cloud-only POS stops when the connection does. Virtual Manager runs fully offline.
  2. Can it handle udhar? Many customers buy on credit. Look for running customer balances, not just cash sales.
  3. Does it support Urdu? Customer names and receipts should look right to the people who read them.
  4. Does it show profit or only sales? Profit needs the real cost of the stock sold, plus expenses.
  5. Can staff use it safely? Roles, permissions and approvals protect your records.
  6. What does it cost after the first year? Check renewal and subscription fees, not just the first price.
