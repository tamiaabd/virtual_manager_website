Sales and Purchase App for Wholesale and Retail

Record what you sold, what you bought and who still owes money. Virtual Manager brings retail and wholesale records, customer credit and supplier balances into one app for Windows and Android.

  • Customer khata
  • Supplier balances
  • PDF statements

Record credit sales and payments in your shop khata

Choose a customer and save the sale with its date and items. If the customer pays part of the bill, record that payment against their account and check the outstanding balance. Record later payments against the same customer so the original sale and each payment remain visible.

For example, on a PKR 5,000 sale with PKR 2,000 paid today, PKR 3,000 is still owed. When the customer brings another PKR 1,000, record a payment and check that the remaining balance is PKR 2,000. You can share a receipt or use a PDF sale statement to review a period together.

Keep supplier balances and stock receipts separate

Record goods when you buy them from a supplier. When those goods should count as stock, add them to inventory. Record what you paid against the supplier separately so you can reconcile the outstanding balance and follow the goods in the stock ledger.

Review a day or a month without retyping totals

Use a date range to look back at sales, purchases and payments. Share PDF statements when you need to compare records with a customer or supplier. Use the profit overview alongside sales totals: stock cost and expenses affect what the business actually earned.

Use the app offline or share access with staff

Start in offline mode without an account. Shopkeepers who want more than a credit-only khata can keep purchases, stock and receipts in the same app. For a shared business workspace, sign in and set up cloud sync and staff permissions.

If you already have records in Excel, preview and import your existing records. For service fees and non-stock sales in the upcoming build, compare the new Sales Types.
