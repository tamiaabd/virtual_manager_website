What’s New in Virtual Manager

Here’s what’s changing in Virtual Manager, from recording services without taking stock to checking import errors and giving staff access to the right records.

  • Sales Types
  • Excel import
  • Ledger Sheet view
  • Staff roles
Release preview · 6 November 2026

The features below describe the upcoming build. The direct Windows and Android downloads still report version 1.9.0 as of this check. The Windows Store edition updates separately and may include some changes already. No release date for Sales Types has been announced here.

Record stock sales, non-stock items and services

Normal sales take stock from inventory using FIFO. Independent sales use a name, quantity and rate without taking stock. Service rows use a service name, a service date and an amount, without stock or quantity. A credit service can still leave a customer balance.

For example, a trader can sell a stocked item, add a non-stock item and charge for delivery on the same sale. Only the stocked item reduces inventory. The service date appears on the receipt, while its revenue follows the sale date in reports.

The new default is Normal only. Owners who previously used Independent Sales can enable it again in Settings → Sales Types. Existing lines keep their type. All three types work in the standalone app; signed-in staff use the types their business allows.

See which Excel rows need a decision

Resolve import errors from the highlighted cell or issue list. Correct the value, leave the row out, or explicitly choose Independent or Service where appropriate. Services require a positive Line Amount and can have their own date. Normal and Independent require quantity and rate; the importer no longer fills missing values for those fields automatically.

Review partial or repeated payments before saving. Purchases can map freight or similar charges to linked expenses so the supplier balance reflects what is owed to the supplier. The original workbook stays unchanged. See the Excel data import guide.

Read the stock ledger like a spreadsheet

The new Sheet view sits alongside Detail and Summary. It puts one movement on each row and keeps headings in view while you scroll. Resize columns to see longer names and export the Sheet view to a landscape PDF. Learn how stock movements are recorded.

Record attendance and pay without closing the month first

Mark attendance, record advances and salary payments, and review a monthly statement. The upcoming workflow supports working hours, days off and holidays, plus a PDF or Excel attendance register. Staff using their own phones can check in via nearby Bluetooth or the manager’s QR code in a connected workspace. Read the attendance guide.

Set staff roles and organise long lists

Save custom roles and choose which modules a staff member can see or edit. You can limit some users to their own entries or to a retail customer. Staff can sign in with a QR code or one-time code. Sort customers and suppliers by name, group wholesale customers by agent, or show sales and purchases grouped by day.

Design or scan your business stamp

Choose a stamp shape and font, preview it on a receipt, or turn a photo of a real stamp into a design for receipts and PDFs. You can also export a print file for a stamp maker.
