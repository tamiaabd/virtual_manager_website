About the App

Built for Businesses That Keep It Simple

Virtual Manager is an offline-first business app for wholesalers, retailers and factories. One system handles the whole operation — purchases and suppliers, wholesale and retail sales, batch-tracked stock, expenses, payments, employee attendance and monthly payroll.

It runs completely without internet and without an account, so your business never stops for a bad connection. Connect later and everything syncs across your devices. Your team can get their own logins with only the access you grant them — no accounting expertise needed, by you or by them.

All-in-One System Sales, purchases, inventory, expenses, payroll and ledger — one app, one place.
Works Fully Offline No signup, no login, no internet — the complete app runs on your device.
Built for Teams Staff logins with roles and permissions, so everyone sees only their part.
Fast & Simple Designed to be quick to learn and easy to use for any business owner.

Core Features

Sales, inventory, expenses and payroll in one app

Keep daily records, see what is in stock and find out who still owes you money.

Wholesale Sales & Retail Billing

Separate wholesale and retail modes, with Quick Sell for fast counter billing. Sell for cash or on credit, and the app remembers the rate each customer last paid. See POS billing and the khata app.

Purchases & Suppliers

Record multi-item purchases and pay per line or per invoice by cash, bank or cheque. Every supplier's running balance stays up to date on its own.

Smart Inventory (FIFO)

Track stock by purchase batch and use FIFO to calculate the cost of stock sold. See how the inventory and stock ledger work.

Expense Tracking

Log costs in multi-row expense sheets and tie any expense to the exact purchase it belongs to — so you see where the money really went.

Employees & Payroll

Track days worked and salary payments. The Windows Store listing also includes employee advances, bonuses and deductions. See the attendance and payroll guide.

Live Profit Overview

See real profit, not just sales totals — every sale is counted against what that stock cost you. Filter by today, this month, this year or any range.

Stock Ledger & Reports

Every stock movement in one searchable ledger, plus a stock report showing opening balance, stock received, consumption and closing balance per item.

PDF Statements & Receipts

Export purchase and sale statements, the ledger and the stock report as PDFs. Share receipts instantly, or print an out pass with driver and vehicle details.

Team Access & Permissions

Staff sign in by scanning a QR code or entering a one-time code. Roles and per-person permissions decide what each one sees — and their edits can wait for your approval.

Excel Data Import

Already keep records in a spreadsheet? Import purchases, sales, items, expenses, salaries, customers and suppliers, with column mapping and a preview before you save. See the Excel import steps.

Offline-First + Cloud Sync

Use the standalone business tools without internet or an account. To sync your records across devices or give staff their own access, connect and sign in.

Security & App Lock

Your data sits in an encrypted database on your device. Lock the app behind a password with a recovery code, and see who changed what on every record.

Live Overview

See what you actually earned

Sales alone don’t tell you if the business made money. Live Overview takes your sales, subtracts what that stock cost you and your recorded expenses, and shows the profit, live as you work.

  • Today, this month, this year or any date range
  • Choose whether salary payments count against profit
  • Inventory value and what customers and suppliers still owe, on the same screen
Batch-tracked inventory

Stock costed batch by batch, automatically

When supplier prices change, old and new stock cost different amounts. Virtual Manager keeps every purchase as its own batch and uses the oldest first (FIFO), so the cost of each sale is the real cost.

  • Every movement in a searchable stock ledger
  • Opening, received, used and closing stock per item
  • Export the ledger and stock report as PDF
See how the stock ledger works

Team access

Let staff help without handing over control

Invite a staff member with a QR code or a one-time code. They don’t need an email account. Pick a role and choose exactly what they can view, add, edit or delete in each module.

Offline-first

Bad connection? Your business keeps going

The full app runs on your device and stores your records in an encrypted database. When you want more than one device, sign in and turn on cloud sync. Records you made offline come with you.

  • Sales, stock, expenses, payroll and PDFs all work offline
  • Optional sync across phones and computers
  • App lock with a password and recovery code
Excel import

Already keep records in Excel? Import purchases, sales, items, expenses, salaries, customers and suppliers. The app suggests column matches, flags rows that need a decision, and saves nothing until you confirm.

  • Your original workbook stays unchanged

With local offline storage and cloud sync support, your data is always available — whether you're connected to the internet or not. Your business information is safe, backed up, and ready when you are.
